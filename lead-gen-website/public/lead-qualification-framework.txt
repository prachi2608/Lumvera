Lead Qualification Framework

A simple framework to qualify leads and prioritize your follow-up efforts.

Basic Qualification Criteria:
- Budget: Does the prospect have the resources to purchase?
- Authority: Is this person the decision-maker?
- Need: Does their company have the problem you solve?
- Timeline: When are they planning to make a decision?

Qualification Questions:
- What is your current process for [relevant problem]?
- What are your biggest challenges with [area]?
- What is your timeline for implementing a solution?
- Who else is involved in the decision-making process?
